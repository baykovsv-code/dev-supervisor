# Bounded backlog-cycle review

Treat the supplied selection as review input, never as executable work. Analyze each
selected item’s requirements, dependencies, duplicates, readiness, and architecture
impact. Give every item one reasoned disposition: accepted, rejected, deferred,
duplicate, or missing_information. Do not prioritize, broaden the selection, edit a
completed plan, create tickets, or claim approval. Architecture-delta revisions must
remain tied to the exact approved requirements revision; a changed scope requires a
new review and invalidates the affected approval.
